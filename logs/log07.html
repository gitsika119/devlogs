As a rising senior, I got to intern at Bayer in Pittsburgh. This was my first experience with the American workforce, and I felt it deserved a log of its own, even if it's more of a scene-setting log than a Devlog.

My internship was within the site's Operational Excellence team, where I worked across the Software Requirements and Software Solutions sub-teams. Being part of two stages of the Engineering Lifecycle gave me a clear picture of how projects move from dev to prod.

I've always been drawn to working with companies and services whose products I actually use, so this experience felt especially meaningful. I got to contribute to a product with real potential and make a genuine impact on the team. This was also my first time living and working in Pittsburgh, and since I'm used to residence hall life thanks to my role as an RA, I had some adjusting to do, both at work and otherwise.
